About YardHarvest

A community marketplace connecting backyard gardeners with people who love fresh, local produce.

How It Works

For Sellers
  1. Create an account as a seller
  2. List your surplus vegetables with photos and prices
  3. Set your pickup location and optional delivery zone
  4. Accept orders and coordinate with buyers
  5. Earn extra income from your garden!
For Buyers
  1. Browse listings near your location
  2. Add items to your cart
  3. Place a "request to buy" order
  4. Coordinate pickup or delivery with the seller
  5. Enjoy fresh, locally grown produce!
About Payments

YardHarvest connects buyers and sellers. Payment is handled directly between you (cash, Venmo, etc.) when you pick up or receive your produce.
