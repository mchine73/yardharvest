{# ── Algorithm Explanation ── #}
How Smart Pricing Works

The smart pricing algorithm adjusts listing prices dynamically based on three factors:

Supply Factor

Low remaining stock drives the price up; full stock pushes it slightly down. Controlled by Supply Weight.

Sales Velocity

Fast recent sales (last 7 days) signal high demand and increase price. Controlled by Velocity Weight.

Time Decay

Older listings with remaining stock get a price reduction to encourage sales. Controlled by Time Decay Weight.

Formula

smart_price = base_price x (1 + weighted_adjustment x global_multiplier)

The result is clamped between floor and ceiling per listing.

Sellers can also opt out of smart pricing per listing.
