🌱 New garden admin guide

Welcome — let's grow something great.

You've got a community garden to run. YardHarvest handles the plots, the people, the payments, and the paperwork — so you can focus on the growing. Here's everything you need to get started, in plain English.

Start the 3-step setup Tour the features

Up and running in three steps

Most admins are ready to welcome their first members in under 30 minutes. Don't worry about getting everything perfect — you can change any of this later.

1

Set up your garden

Add your name, location, season dates, and garden rules under Settings. This becomes your public garden page that new members find and apply to.

2

Add your plots

Create your plots with sizes and locations, then arrange them on the visual layout grid. Mark each as available, assigned, reserved, or under maintenance.

3

Open the gates

Share your public page, approve members from your waitlist, and post a welcome announcement. You're officially running a garden on YardHarvest.

Tips for a thriving first season

A few habits that the best garden admins swear by.

✍️

Write clear garden rules

Set expectations early — plot upkeep, shared tools, water use. Members appreciate knowing the ground rules from day one.

📨

Post a weekly note

A short announcement each week keeps the garden connected. Workday reminders and harvest highlights work great.

📸

Share photos often

Photos on your public page attract new applicants and celebrate your members' hard work. A picture really does pull people in.

🗓️

Plan a season kickoff

An opening workday is the easiest way to build community. Create the event, let members RSVP, and you're set.

💧

Track shared resources

Log your tools and hoses so nothing wanders off. Pro adds checkout so you always know who has what.

🏦

Connect payouts early

Link your Stripe account before dues season so collected money lands straight in your garden's bank account.

Common questions

Do I have to pay to run a garden?

No. Opening a garden, adding plots, approving members, posting announcements, and accepting payouts are all free. Garden Pro is optional and adds advanced tools when you want them.

How do new members find my garden?

Every garden gets a public page where people can browse and apply. Share the link, and applications land in your waitlist for review.

How do members pay dues?

Connect your garden's Stripe account once, and dues are paid online and deposited directly to your bank. Dues tracking and CSV export are part of Garden Pro.

Can I change my garden details later?

Absolutely. Everything — rules, season dates, plots, layout — can be edited any time from your admin dashboard.

What is AI moderation?

Public comments on your community wall are automatically screened for spam and abuse before they appear. Anything questionable is flagged for you to approve or remove.
