🤖 AI BDR · human in the middle

An AI BDR that does the legwork — you make the calls.

The agent works your pipeline the way a sales-development rep would: it finds who to contact, drafts the outreach, and proposes the next move. Then it stops and waits for you. Every email, every campaign, every status change happens only when you approve it.

🛡️ Nothing is sent, created, or changed until you click Approve.

How it works: propose → approve → execute

Everything the agent does runs through one approval queue. You stay in control; the agent does the typing.

1

It scans your leads and drafts the next step — each with a plain-English reason and an editable draft.

→
2

Proposals collect in the Approval queue on the BDR Agent page. Nothing has happened yet.

→
3

Read the rationale, tweak the subject/body, or change your mind. You’re the editor-in-chief.

→
✓

Approve → it executes

Approve and the agent sends, logs it to the timeline, and advances the lead. Or Dismiss it.

Four jobs, one approval queue

Open the BDR Agent page in the CRM and use the buttons up top. Each kind of proposal shows up in the same queue, styled so you can tell them apart at a glance.

Scout

Find who to work

Drawn only from real leads already in your CRM — it never invents organizations.

Engage

Personalized with merge tokens so every recipient sees their own name, org, and city.
Approve = Approve & send → sends, logs it, advances the lead, and schedules the next touch in 4 days.

Open/click rates and per-recipient engagement show on the campaign’s page.
Approve = Create draft campaign → review the audience, then send (a deliberate second step for a blast).

The lead lifecycle the agent works through

Each contact has a status the agent (and you) move it through — separate from a deal’s pipeline stage, which only begins once a lead is qualified.

New→ Working→ Engaged→ Qualified→ Customer · DisqualifiedNurture

The first three are “open” — actively prospected, and what the agent pulls from. Hit Qualify → create deal on a contact to hand it into the sales pipeline.

A day with the agent

A simple rhythm: scout in the morning, work the queue, let follow-ups keep leads warm.

1

Open the Lead Queue

See who’s due now — new leads and follow-ups that have come due — filtered by status or owner.

2

Scout fresh prospects

On the BDR Agent page, click Scout leads. Approve the picks worth pursuing; they drop into your working queue.

3

Run follow-ups

Click Run follow-ups, skim each draft, tweak anything, and Approve & send. The agent logs and reschedules.

4

Log calls & meetings

On a contact, record a call or meeting with its outcome. The lead advances and the next action is set.

5

Send a campaign

Propose campaign, review the draft and audience, then send — and watch opens and clicks roll in.

6

Qualify & hand off

When a lead’s ready, hit Qualify → create deal to move it into the sales pipeline.

What keeps it safe

The agent is fast, not autonomous. A few hard rules keep you — and your sender reputation — protected.

🛡️
You approve everything

No email, campaign, or status change happens without your click. Dismiss anything you don’t like.

✍️
No fabrication

The agent works only from real data already in your CRM. It never invents organizations, people, or facts.

📣
Mass sends are a second step

An approved campaign becomes a draft. You review the audience and press Send yourself.

🚫
Opt-outs respected

Unsubscribed and suppressed addresses are skipped automatically on every send.

🔌
AI is optional

Drafting needs an AI key (ANTHROPIC_API_KEY). Without it the buttons grey out — the queue still works.

🧭
Everything is logged

Each approved action lands on the contact’s timeline, so there’s always a record of what went out.
